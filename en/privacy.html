Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Stop, Stop! (“the app”) is a game for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad made by Alysoft. The short version is simple: the app does not collect, send to any server, sell or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts and no servers. It does not ask for your email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Local network

To play, the table (an Apple TV or an iPad) and the players' iPhones or iPads talk to each other directly over your own Wi‑Fi network. That is why the iPhone and iPad app asks for the Local Network permission: to find the table or, on an iPad, to be it. What travels between your devices (each player's name, the answers, the categories, the challenges and the votes) stays on your local network, is never sent to the internet or to us, and is gone when the table closes.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps, only on that device: on a phone, the name you typed, your animal, your own categories and a random code that gives you your seat back if your connection drops; on the table, the sets of settings you saved. This data never leaves the device, does not identify you, and is deleted if you delete the app.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Stop, Stop! Complete is an in-app purchase made through the App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. They contain no personal information and are used only to fix bugs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to stopstop@alysoft.com.br.
